Before you speak with a lawyer

  • Write down key dates

    Make a brief timeline. Include any upcoming hearings or deadlines you know about.

  • Gather relevant records

    Make a list of documents or notices you have. Ask how to share them securely before sending copies.

  • Bring your questions

    Think about the outcome you hope for and what you want to understand about the process, timing, or fees.

Frequently asked questions

What if I am unsure which kind of lawyer I need?

Describe the issue in a sentence or two and ask whether it falls within the lawyer's practice.

What can I ask about fees?

Ask whether there is a fee for the initial conversation, how the lawyer bills, and what expenses may come up.

How should I share documents?

Ask the office which records it needs and how it accepts them securely. Keep originals for your records unless the office requests them.
